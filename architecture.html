03 · National capability architecture

Six systems.
One national architecture.

Explore the capability domains and their dependencies. The model is designed around real relationships documented across the programme corpus—not decorative connections.

Selected capability

Energy sovereignty

Firm, clean and dispatchable power is the enabling layer for industry, compute, water, mobility, health and strategic resilience.

  • Advanced nuclear and SMR pathways
  • Grid, microgrid and storage integration
  • Industrial heat, hydrogen and water
RELATIONSHIP
Enables infrastructure, industrial depth, technology and resilient public services.
The organising logic

Interdependence is the strategy.

No capability domain is sovereign in isolation.

Energy without industry exports value. Infrastructure without institutions degrades. Technology without secure power becomes dependency. Governance without evidence converts ambition into uncontrolled risk. Architecture makes those relationships governable.

Programme domains

Architecture applied at
sovereign scale.

The architecture becomes tangible through six programme domains.

Each domain can stand alone. Their strategic value compounds when energy, cities, compute, science, industry and resilience are designed around a shared national mission.

01 · Energy systems

Advanced nuclear & sovereign energy

Microreactors, high-temperature gas reactors, industrial heat, hydrogen and gas-to-nuclear pathways—developed through controlled technical and regulatory gates.

Foundation layer · Enables all downstream systems
02 · Urban systems

Platform cities

Energy, mobility, buildings, health, data, finance and citizen services integrated as one operating urban platform.

Spatial layer · Converts infrastructure into daily capability
03 · Digital systems

Sovereign compute

Secure compute ecosystems aligned with firm power, cooling, water, cyber resilience and national control of critical data.

Intelligence layer · Measures and coordinates the system
04 · Scientific systems

National laboratories

Mission-led institutions connecting frontier research, technical workforce formation, testing, standards and commercial deployment.

Knowledge layer · Converts research into national competence
05 · Industrial systems

Industrial transformation

Energy-intensive manufacturing, green steel, advanced materials and domestic supply chains built around reliable power.

Productive layer · Retains value, skills and strategic capacity
06 · Resilience systems

Strategic resilience

Mission-critical campuses combining secure energy, communications, logistics, water and continuity of operations.

Continuity layer · Protects essential national functions
