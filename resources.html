Evidence taxonomy

Ambition must never
outrun evidence.

Every consequential statement should carry an evidence meaning.

The taxonomy prevents corporate propositions, controlled programme baselines and technical targets from being misread as independently demonstrated performance.

01

Verified fact

Supported by primary evidence or independent confirmation appropriate to the claim.

Decision-grade where current
02

Controlled baseline

An approved programme assumption with a named owner, status and revision.

Bounded design use
03

Performance target

An intended technical, financial or delivery outcome that has not been demonstrated.

Validation required
04

Strategic proposition

A stated institutional position, ambition, differentiation or future-state thesis.

Not external proof
05

Concept

An illustrative option under assessment, before complete substantiation or approval.

Not a commitment

What was
reviewed.

The working corpus contains 121 files organised across eight programme areas.

Sources include 52 DOCX files, 60 PDFs, eight presentations and one engineering SVG. Programme areas include TFOR, TMOR, HTGR SMR, gas-to-nuclear, fusion, data centres, military platforms and supporting institutional materials. Duplicate format editions may represent the same underlying work.

RuleApplication
CurrencyUse document revision, issue date and evidence cut-off where stated.
AttributionSeparate official corporate claims from independent public records.
GeographyCountry material does not itself prove approval, contract or deployment.
Technical maturityUse the maturity language in the source; do not infer operating performance.
Financial valuesTreat projections and capital requirements as forward-looking until verified.
